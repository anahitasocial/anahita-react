## What we collect

**When you create an account:** a display name, a handle, an email address and
a password. The password is stored only as a bcrypt hash — we cannot read it,
and neither can an administrator.

**When you accept these terms:** the date, the version you accepted, your IP
address, your browser's user-agent string and your operating system. This is
recorded so we can show what was agreed and when. It is the reason the record
is worth anything; without it we would have a tick-box and no evidence.

**When you sign in:** the time, your IP address, the approximate city and
country it resolves to, and the device and browser. You can see this list in
your security settings and end any session from there.

**What you put on your profile:** anything you choose to add — a photo, a
biography, pronouns, a link. All of it is optional, and what you put on a
public profile is public.

**What you post:** your posts, comments, messages, and who you follow.

**Technical logs:** ordinary web-server records of requests, kept for a short
period for security and debugging.

## What we use it for

- To run your account and sign you in.
- To show your content to the people you shared it with.
- To send you the notifications you have asked for, and service messages such
  as a password reset. Service messages are not optional while you have an
  account.
- To keep the Service secure, and to investigate abuse.
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for
advertising.

## What other people can see

Your display name, handle, profile picture and anything you mark public are
visible to anyone who can reach the site, including search engines.

Your email address is not shown to other members.

**If this installation federates** with other servers, your public profile and
public posts are sent to those servers and stored there. We cannot control or
delete those copies. Deleting something here asks other servers to remove
theirs, and most honour it, but it is a request rather than a guarantee.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account and its content:** until you delete it.
- **Deleted accounts:** a grace period during which signing in restores the
  account, then deletion. The profile row itself is kept as a tombstone with
  its content removed, so the handle cannot be re-issued to somebody else —
  this protects you from being impersonated after you leave.
- **Sign-in history:** a rolling window, oldest entries dropped.
- **Agreement records:** kept while the account exists and for as long
  afterwards as we may need to show what was agreed.
- **Backups:** expire on their own schedule, typically within
  **[retention period]**.

## Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Two-factor authentication and passkeys are
available, and TOTP secrets are encrypted at rest. Traffic is served over HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and is
likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the relevant authority
**[within 72 hours, where GDPR applies]**.
